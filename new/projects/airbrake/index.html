Overview

I was on UF's Swamp Launch Rocket Team for two years. I designed the deployment mechanism for the rocket's airbrake.

Approach

The mechanism uses a cam and linkage to slide the brake panels straight out of the airframe. I designed the mechanism and built about half of the CAD assembly.

Results

The airbrake flew at Spaceport America Cup.
