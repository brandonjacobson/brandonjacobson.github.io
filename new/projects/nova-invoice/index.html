Overview

Nova Invoice started as a SwampHacks XI hackathon project. It won MLH Best Use of Solana. I turned it into a company, Nova Invoice LLC.

Businesses send invoices and get paid in crypto. The platform is non-custodial, so it never holds funds. It's live at novafinancial.xyz.

Approach

Payments are live in SOL, ETH, USDT and USDC. Subscriptions run on Stripe billing with three tiers. The codebase has an automated suite of 348 tests.

What didn't work

Background jobs ran on BullMQ. They drove about 30M Redis operations a month against a cap of 500k. I replaced every queue-based job with interval sweeps, then removed BullMQ and ioredis entirely.

I also fixed a Safari bug that wiped auth tokens. During a full mobile responsiveness pass, I fixed an iOS Safari SVG rendering bug.
